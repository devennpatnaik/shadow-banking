# Simulator

The shadow-bank simulator (Phase 2). Models the recursive collateral-and-borrowing loop for at least two candidate asset ecosystems, computing leverage, net yield, equity, and liquidation risk at each step, including collateral haircuts, liquidation thresholds, borrowing limits, and liquidity constraints. Also hosts the ~$1,000 feasibility assessment: whether the economics work at seed scale once transaction costs and minimum-size constraints are included.
